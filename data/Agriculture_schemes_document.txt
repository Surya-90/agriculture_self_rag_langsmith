4. Minimum support price scheme

Minimum price for a product,established by a government and supported by payments to producers in the event that the market price falls below the specified minimum price.
A price support scheme can also be an agreement set in order by the government, where the government agrees to purchase the surplus of at a minimum price.
For example, if a price floor were set in place for paddy, the government would be forced to purchase the resulting surplus from the paddy farmers (thereby subsidizing the farmers) and store or otherwise dispose it.
The price support scheme is basically to ensure farmers a minimum support price which is worked out by the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) set up by the Government of India.
In the procurement operation, National Agricultural Co-operative Marketing Federation -NAFED appoints state level agencies in consultation with the State Governments who in turn involve their co-operative marketing societies for the procurement at the field level.
This scheme safeguards the farmers' interest especially during periods of high production and crash in price.

1. YSR Jagananna Saswatha Bhoomi Hakku-Bhoo Rakshana
Scheme
YSR Jagananna Saswatha Bhoomi Hakku-Bhoo Rakshana scheme is launched for land
protection of the residents of the state. It tends to protect the entitled ownership and
rights of the residents. This scheme was initially announced and the pilot project was
started in Krishna district in a village named Thakkellapadu. On December 21, 2020,
CM officially laid the boundary stone for this pilot project. This scheme is basically
launched to remove the irregularities and bring transparency in land matters. It is
mainly a solution for maintaining the records related to ownerships of land properties.
It will tend to reduce the property disputes which are prevalent. On April 22, 2021 CM
held a review meeting regarding the scheme so as to ensure the process is corruption
free and easy for farmers.
Scheme Objectives and Benefits:
 The scheme aims to claim ownership and maintain records of all the residential
property owned
 Land titling card will be a proof of ownership to the property holders
 These cards can be produced as security and this will enable the holder to get loan
 Use of technology in villages will lead to rural and urban development
 It aims to reducing disputes in villages over property issues
 Property ownership will give the rural youth self-confidence thereby making them
self-reliant
Key Points:
 YSR Jagananna Saswatha Bhoomi Hakku-Bhoo Rakshana Scheme is launched by the
Andhra Pradesh government mainly for tracing property ownership and reducing
the property disputes in rural areas
 This scheme is implemented through Survey of Villages and Mapping with
Improvised Modern Technology and Drone Technology
 Under this scheme land titling cards will be issued to the property owners after
tracing the ownership
 The scheme was initially implemented in Thakkellapadu village in Krishna district,
as a pilot project
 About 4500 survey teams are set up for this scheme
 Under this scheme, the property owner should reach out to the department with all
the documents entitling the details of the land along with Aadhaar card, then proper
location of the property will be traced and based on verification and social audit, the
land title will be given to the owner
 With the implementation of this scheme the land owner will get the legal rights of
his property even if he doesn’t have the papers/ documents to claim his property
 CM held a review meeting on April 22, 2021 on this scheme to ensure things are
going well and the process is corruption free
 He also advised the officials to ensure accuracy during the survey
 Sub registrar offices will be set up in the villages where land survey is completed
 Currently land survey in 51 villages will be completed by the month of July

 For applying Online, the applicant has to visit @ ysrjalakala.ap.gov.in

 Enter Aadhaar no. and OTP for verification
 Then the application form is displayed
 Enter the required details like district, village, panchayat, etc
 Then fill in land details, total acres owned, etc and submit

secretariat
 After verification of the submitted form, a drilling contractor will be assigned for

 For this survey, a qualified geologist will be appointed and he will be responsible
to submit a feasibility report to the concerned ADP/MPDO for generating estimate
 PD will ten take an approval from District Collector/JC, Aasara
 Digital photograph with geotag will be taken by the concerned authorities along

Uploaded ImageIntroduction
The Rajiv Gandhi Entrepreneurship Program (RGEP) is a startup support initiative launched by the Government of Karnataka to encourage innovation and entrepreneurship among young Science and Engineering graduates. The programme is implemented through the Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society (KITS) under the Department of Electronics, Information Technology, Biotechnology, and Science & Technology. It aims to help aspiring entrepreneurs transform innovative ideas into viable businesses by providing financial assistance, mentorship, and industry support during the early stages of startup development.

Through this programme, selected innovators receive financial backing to focus full-time on their ideas without the immediate pressure of generating revenue or seeking employment. The scheme primarily supports innovations at the ideation, prototyping, or proof-of-concept stage and helps entrepreneurs build scalable, commercially sustainable solutions. In addition to funding, participants gain access to startup ecosystem partners, networking opportunities, expert guidance, workshops, and market validation support.

The programme follows a competitive selection process where applications are evaluated based on innovation, problem-solving potential, social impact, feasibility, and commercial viability. Eligible candidates can submit their applications online via the Startup Karnataka portal, providing details of their proposed innovation, a pitch deck, and supporting documents.

Benefits
Monthly financial assistance of ₹25,000 for 12 months.
Total grant support of up to ₹3 lakh per selected innovator.
Mentorship from designated Program Monitoring Partners (PMPs).
Guidance for developing a Prototype, MVP, or Proof of Concept.
Regular one-on-one mentoring and progress review sessions.
Access to startup workshops, industry experts, and networking events.
Support for market research, validation, business strategy, and pitching.
Exposure to investors and startup ecosystem stakeholders.
Structured milestone-based entrepreneurial development programme.
Eligibility
Applicant must be an Indian citizen and domicile of Karnataka.
Must possess a 3-year or 4-year degree in Science or Engineering.
Age should not exceed 30 years at the time of application.
Should not be employed in any organization for remuneration.
Must not hold ownership or investment in another startup.
Must commit full-time to the proposed startup idea for 12 months.
Innovation should be in the ideation, validation, or early prototype stage.
Commercialised products or revenue-generating startups are not eligible.
Total grants received for the proposed idea should not exceed ₹5 lakh.
The product, service, or solution should not have been launched in the market.
Documents
Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID as proof of Indian citizenship.
Karnataka Domicile Certificate, Ration Card, or Voter ID.
Science or Engineering Degree Certificate.
Self-declaration regarding non-involvement in other startups.
Completed RGEP Pitch Deck.
Recent passport-size photograph.
Any additional documents requested during evaluation.

Download and prepare the prescribed pitch deck.
Click on the "Apply Now" option.
Fill in personal, educational, and startup idea details.
Upload the required documents and pitch deck.
Review all information and submit the application online.
Eligible applicants will be shortlisted for jury evaluation and presentation.
Selected innovators will be onboarded into the programme after approval.

It is a startup support programme that provides financial assistance, mentorship, and business development support to young innovators in Karnataka.

Selected innovators receive ₹25,000 per month for 12 months, totaling ₹3 lakh.

Q3. Who can apply for the programme?
Science or Engineering graduates from Karnataka aged 30 years or below who have an innovative startup idea can apply.

No. Applicants should not be employed or receiving remuneration from any organization during the programme period.

Introduction
Gruha Jyothi Scheme is a flagship electricity subsidy programme launched by the Government of Karnataka to provide financial relief to domestic consumers across the state. The scheme was introduced in 2023 with the objective of reducing the burden of electricity expenses on households and ensuring affordable access to power for eligible families. Under the scheme, beneficiaries can avail free electricity benefits for consumption up to 200 units per month, subject to the prescribed conditions.

The initiative is one of the major welfare programmes implemented by the Karnataka Government and is aimed at supporting low-income, middle-income, and economically vulnerable households. By eliminating or significantly reducing monthly electricity bills, the scheme helps families save money and improve their overall financial stability. The benefit is available only for domestic electricity connections and does not extend to commercial or industrial consumers.

To receive the benefit, consumers must complete registration through the official Seva Sindhu Portal or designated offline service centres. The scheme is also open to eligible tenants residing in rented accommodations, ensuring wider coverage among households. Once approved, the applicable subsidy is automatically reflected in the monthly electricity bill.

In addition to providing free electricity, the government regularly verifies beneficiary records to ensure that benefits reach genuine consumers. The scheme continues to benefit lakhs of households across Karnataka and remains one of the state's most impactful social welfare initiatives focused on reducing household expenditure and improving access to essential services.

Introduction
The Gruha Lakshmi Scheme is a flagship women's empowerment initiative launched by the Government of Karnataka in 2023 to provide direct financial support to women heads of families. Under this scheme, eligible women receive financial assistance of ₹2,000 per month through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system. The scheme aims to improve the economic stability of households by recognising the contribution of women in managing family responsibilities and ensuring they have access to regular financial assistance.

The benefit is available to women listed as the head of the family in eligible ration cards issued by the Food and Civil Supplies Department. Married, widowed, divorced, and deserted women can apply if they meet the prescribed conditions. The financial assistance is credited directly to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account, ensuring transparency and timely payments.

To strengthen beneficiary records and remove duplicate or ineligible entries, the Karnataka Government has initiated a fresh re-verification drive. The verification process commenced on 30 August 2026 and is expected to continue for approximately one month through door-to-door visits and designated service centres. Eligible beneficiaries are advised to cooperate with the verification process to ensure uninterrupted receipt of benefits.

Benefits
Financial assistance of ₹2,000 per month.
Annual benefit of ₹24,000 for eligible women.
Amount transferred directly to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts through DBT.
Can be used for household expenses, healthcare, education, nutrition, and other family needs.
Provides financial security and economic support to women heads of households.
Eligibility
Applicant must be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
Applicant must be registered as the head of the family in the ration card.
Family should possess an Antyodaya, BPL, or APL ration card.
Married, widowed, divorced, and deserted women are eligible.
Only one woman per family can receive benefits.
Applicant must have an Aadhaar-linked bank account.
Beneficiaries may be required to complete re-verification as directed by the Government.
Who is Not Eligible?
Women whose spouse or family pays Income Tax.
Women whose spouse or family files GST returns.
Duplicate, fake, migrated, deceased, or otherwise ineligible beneficiaries identified during verification.
